# Vidhana Soudha street pass

Scope: 500 m radius around 77.5908, 12.9798. OSM extract fetched 2026-09-10 from the official map API. Attribution: OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL.

Generated by prepare-vidhana-streets.py. Roads and footways follow OSM geometry; measured width tags take priority, missing widths use lane-count or road-class estimates. Stripe locations only use crossing:markings=zebra. Lane spacing and dash lengths are illustrative, not surveyed. Bridges/tunnels excluded from the flat street layer. Round joins connect road surfaces at intersections. Memorials are location markers pending accurate sculpture assets.

Mapped snapshot: 160 road ways, 215 pedestrian/cycle ways, 54 crossing nodes including 21 explicitly zebra-marked crossings, 7 signal nodes, 22 memorial/statue nodes.
