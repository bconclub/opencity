# Bengaluru Living City

Thesis: explore the actual spatial fabric of Bengaluru at city and building scale. Map is the experience; controls recede.
Own world: survey-model slate, pale mineral buildings, deep botanical green, lake blue. System sans for compact operation. One accent for actions.
Story: arrive above central Bengaluru, choose a district, descend, inspect a footprint, orbit, save a camera view.
First viewport: full-screen tilted city; compact left control rail; district title at bottom. Primary action is choosing a place. No marketing page.
Form: streamed OpenFreeMap vector tiles in MapLibre. Honest schematic building heights; no invented simulation results.

Spacing: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32. Controls minimum 44px. Border depth only. Desktop rail becomes a compact top tray on mobile. Reduced motion disables continuous orbit and camera flights.
Tokens: ink #18302d, muted #52655e, panel #f4f7f3, border #c4cfc7, accent #215e46, canvas #dde5dc, water #91c4d0, park #b0c8a0, building #d8d8c7.
No fabricated building heights presented as measured. No traffic, drainage, terrain or structural claims. Always retain map attribution.
